Getting to Bend

Getting to Bend isn’t too difficult. Fly into Portland International Airport and enjoy a scenic three-hour drive through the Cascades, or fly directly into Redmond Municipal Airport, just 25 minutes from the house(s).

  • PDX, Portland International Airport. About a 3 hr drive to Bend.
  • RDM, Redmond Municipal Airport. About 25 min from the house(s).
Getting to the courses

We'll drive to the courses. A few of the more local people are driving the whole way to Bend. We'll coordinate if anyone would need to rent a car.
