Round Formats

Round 1 : 2 Man Best Ball

Both players play their own ball. The team keeps the lower net score on each hole, with strokes from your course handicap on the stroke holes only.

Round 2 : 2 Man Scramble

Both players hit each shot, then play from the better ball until the hole is finished. No handicap — the team posts one gross score.

Round 3 : Singles Match Play

Head-to-head. Each player plays their own ball, and strokes are the difference between the two handicaps in the match.

Round 4 : Individual Stroke Play

Play your own ball and post an 18-hole total. Net score is gross minus your course handicap.

Handicap

Best ball, singles matches, and the final stroke play round are handicapped. Strokes can only be taken on the associated handicap hole.

Tie breakers

Individual match ties split points evenly.

Team Competition. Weekend point total ties are broken by:

  1. Team that won most singles matches
  2. Team with the most points in Round 4 (stroke play)
  3. Team Round 4 net total

Individual Champion. Weekend point total ties are broken by:

  1. Round 4 net score
  2. Round 3 and 4 net score
  3. Round 4 gross

Getting Started

  1. Download Golf GameBook. Download the free Golf GameBook app from the App Store.
  2. Create a Free Account. Sign up using your email address or preferred login method. No paid membership is required.
  3. Set Up Your Profile. Add your current Handicap Index to your player profile. This is important because we’ll be using handicaps for several rounds throughout the weekend. Please make sure your handicap is accurate and up to date before arriving.
  4. Join the Tournament. I’ll be creating our Red vs. Blue tournament and sending everyone an invitation through Golf GameBook approximately one week before the trip. Accept the invitation to join the tournament and get set up before arriving. Teams will remain unassigned until our official team reveal on Night 1, when we’ll decide the Red vs. Blue matchups!
