Handicap

Singles matches and the final stroke play round are handicapped. Strokes can only be taken on the associated handicap hole.

Tie breakers

Individual match ties split points evenly.

Team competition. Weekend point total ties are broken by:

  1. Team that won most singles matches
  2. Team with the most points in Round 4 (stroke play)
  3. Team Round 4 net total

Individual champion. Weekend point total ties are broken by:

  1. Round 4 net score
  2. Round 3 and 4 net score
  3. Round 4 gross
